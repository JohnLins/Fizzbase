Startup Hiring Using Fizzbase

Use AI people search to find engineers, operators, and leaders in the Bay Area who fit your startup's stage, stack, and culture.

Startup hiring is different—your search should be too

Job boards and broad LinkedIn searches favor people who are actively looking. The best startup hires are often already employed—people who've shipped at the right stage, have the right technical depth, and would consider a move for the right opportunity. Fizzbase is an AI-powered people search engine for the San Francisco Bay Area that lets you describe the candidate you want in natural language and get a ranked list of people who match, whether or not they're on the job market.

Describe the person, not just the role

With Fizzbase you can search for things like: full-stack engineers who have worked at YC startups and have 3+ years in React, first PMs who scaled a product from zero to 100k users at a Series A, or ops leads who have run support and success at high-growth B2B startups. The system uses deep agentic AI over a Bay Area people graph to surface candidates by experience, skills, and ecosystem—so you spend time talking to the right people instead of sifting through noise.

Why startups use Fizzbase for recruiting

  • Stage and culture fit: Find people who've already thrived at your stage (pre-seed, Series A, growth).
  • Technical and functional precision: Go beyond job titles to skills, stack, and track record.
  • Bay Area focus: Index is built for SF and the Bay—where most startup talent lives.
  • Speed: Results in seconds, not days of filter-tweaking or recruiter back-and-forth.
