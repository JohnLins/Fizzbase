Why GTM teams need AI people search

Go-to-market success depends on reaching the right people: buyers, champions, and decision-makers who fit your ideal customer profile. Generic list-building tools give you titles and companies, but not the nuanced fit that makes outreach convert. Fizzbase is an AI-powered people search engine that lets you describe your ideal GTM target in plain language and get a ranked list of high-signal contacts in the San Francisco Bay Area.

From ICP to live list in one search

Instead of juggling filters on LinkedIn or Apollo, you tell Fizzbase what you want: e.g. VP Sales at Series B SaaS companies in SF who previously scaled outbound at a unicorn, or eng leaders who adopted modern devtools in the last 18 months. The system uses deep agentic AI over a Bay Area people graph to return a shortlist of people who actually match, with context on role, company, and ecosystem—so your GTM team can prioritize who to contact first.
