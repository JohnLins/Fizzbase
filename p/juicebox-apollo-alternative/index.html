Why look for an alternative to Juicebox and Apollo?

Juicebox and Apollo are built around filters and keywords: you pick job titles, companies, industries, and hope the combination returns the right people. That works for broad lists but often misses the nuance—e.g. "engineers who've shipped at early-stage AI startups" or "sales leaders who scaled outbound at PLG companies." Fizzbase is an alternative that uses AI people search: you describe the person you want in plain language and get a ranked set of matches from a deep Bay Area people graph.

How Fizzbase is different from Juicebox and Apollo

  • Natural language: No dropdowns or Boolean logic—you type what you're looking for and the AI finds it.
  • Deep intent matching: Fizzbase uses agentic AI to understand experience, context, and ecosystem fit, not just title/company.
  • Bay Area focus: Index and ranking are tuned for the San Francisco Bay Area, so results are relevant to the ecosystem you care about.
  • Speed: Queries run in seconds on a pre-built people graph.

When to use Fizzbase instead of Juicebox or Apollo

Use Fizzbase when you need precision over volume: recruiting for a specific profile, building a targeted outbound list, or mapping people in a niche. Use it when your ideal person is defined by experience and context as much as by title. Try it at fizzbase.com and see how it compares to Juicebox and Apollo for your use case.
